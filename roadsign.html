Road Signs

Learn road signs before taking your driver's license test.

Stop

Come to a complete stop before proceeding.

Zebra Crossing

A zebra crossing road sign indicates a pedestrian crossing zone marked with black‑and‑white stripes for safe road crossing.

Speed Limit

A speed limit road sign indicates the maximum legal driving speed allowed on that section of the road.

No U Turn

A "No U‑Turn" road sign indicates that drivers are prohibited from making a U‑turn at that location.

No Left Turn

A "No Left Turn" road sign indicates that vehicles are prohibited from turning left at that intersection or junction.

No Overtaking

A "No Overtaking" road sign indicates that drivers are prohibited from passing or overtaking other vehicles on that stretch of road.

No Parking

A "No Parking" road sign indicates that vehicles are not allowed to stop or park in the designated area.

Men at work

A "Men at Work" road sign indicates that road construction or maintenance is in progress ahead, warning drivers to slow down and proceed with caution.

Max Speed

Maximum speed allowed.

Two Way Traffic

A "Two‑Way Traffic" road sign indicates that vehicles will be moving in both directions on the road ahead.

Horn Prohibited

A "Horn Prohibited" road sign indicates that drivers are not allowed to use their vehicle horns in that area.

Parking

A "Parking" road sign indicates that vehicles are permitted to stop and park in the designated area.

Restaurant

A "Restaurant" road sign indicates the presence of a nearby dining facility where food and refreshments are available.

Rest Room

A "Rest Room" road sign indicates the availability of nearby public toilet facilities for travelers.

Hotel

A "Hotel" road sign indicates the presence of nearby lodging or accommodation facilities for travelers.

Hospital

A "Hospital" road sign indicates the presence of nearby medical facilities where healthcare services are available.

Hotline

A "Hotline" road sign indicates the presence of an emergency or help phone service available nearby for immediate assistance.

Crossing Road

A "Crossing Road" sign indicates a designated pedestrian crossing area where people may safely walk across the road.

Left Turn

A "Left Turn Ahead" road sign warns drivers that the road will curve or turn to the left shortly, so they should slow down and prepare to navigate the turn safely.

Left Reverse Turn Ahead

A "Left Reverse Turn Ahead" road sign warns drivers that the road will curve sharply to the left in a reverse bend, meaning the turn is more pronounced than a simple curve. It alerts drivers to slow down and prepare for a sharp leftward change in direction.

Right Curve Ahead

A "Right Curve Ahead" road sign warns drivers that the road will bend to the right shortly. It signals them to reduce speed and prepare to steer smoothly through the curve.

Side Road Left

A "Side Road Left" road sign warns drivers that a smaller road will join or intersect from the left side ahead. It alerts them to watch for vehicles entering or crossing from that direction.

Winding Road Left Ahead

A "Winding Road Left Ahead" road sign warns drivers that the road will curve leftward multiple times in succession. It alerts them to slow down and be prepared for continuous bends rather than a single turn.

Slippery When Wet

A "Slippery When Wet" road sign warns drivers that the road surface ahead may become slippery in rainy or wet conditions. It signals them to reduce speed, drive carefully, and maintain a safe distance from other vehicles to avoid skidding.

Signel Ahead

A "Signal Ahead" road sign warns drivers that traffic lights are coming up ahead. It alerts them to slow down, be prepared to stop, and follow the upcoming traffic signals.

Road Narrow

A "Road Narrow" road sign warns drivers that the road ahead will become narrower than the current section. It signals them to reduce speed, stay alert, and be cautious of oncoming traffic since the space for vehicles will be limited.

Lane Reduction From Right

A "Lane Reduction from Right" road sign warns drivers that the number of lanes ahead will decrease, specifically from the right side. It signals them to merge safely to the left, watch for vehicles shifting lanes, and adjust speed to maintain smooth traffic flow.

Hill

A "Hill" road sign warns drivers that there is a steep slope ahead, either uphill or downhill. It alerts them to adjust speed, gear, and braking accordingly to maintain control and safety while navigating the incline.

Hair Pin Curve

A "Hairpin Curve" road sign warns drivers of a very sharp bend ahead, resembling the shape of a hairpin. It signals them to slow down significantly and prepare for a tight turn, often found in hilly or mountainous roads.

Lane Added From Right

A "Lane Added from Right" road sign informs drivers that a new lane will be joining the main roadway from the right side ahead. Unlike a lane reduction, this sign indicates extra space for traffic flow, so drivers should be aware of merging vehicles entering from the right.

Curve To The Right

A "Curve to the Right" road sign warns drivers that the road ahead will bend gently to the right. It signals them to reduce speed slightly, stay alert, and steer smoothly through the curve to maintain control and safety.

Crossroad

A "Crossroad" road sign warns drivers that another road intersects the main road ahead, forming a cross‑shaped junction. It alerts them to slow down, watch for crossing traffic, and be prepared to yield or stop as necessary.

Circular Intersection

A "Circular Intersection" road sign warns drivers that a roundabout is ahead. It signals them to slow down, yield to traffic already in the circle, and navigate carefully through the circular flow of vehicles.

Added Lane from Left

An "Added Lane from Left" road sign informs drivers that a new lane will be joining the main roadway from the left side ahead. Unlike a merge, this lane addition means traffic flow gains extra space, so drivers should be aware of vehicles entering from the left but don’t need to merge themselves.

Advisory Speed

An "Advisory Speed" road sign indicates the recommended safe speed for driving through a specific section of road, such as a curve, hill, or construction zone. Unlike a regulatory speed limit, it’s not legally enforceable but serves as guidance to help drivers navigate safely under normal conditions. It’s usually shown on a yellow sign with black numbers.

Safe Driving Tip

Understanding road signs is the first step to becoming a safe and responsible driver.
