Child Safety Lock (Child Lock)

Cars are equipped with many safety technologies designed to keep both the driver and passengers safe. Among these features is the Child Lock, which is especially designed to protect passengers, particularly children.

Cars are equipped with many safety technologies designed to keep both the driver and passengers safe. Among these features is the Child Lock, which is especially designed to protect passengers, particularly children.

A Child Lock is a safety feature that prevents the rear doors from being opened from the inside when it is switched ON (locked). The doors can still be opened from the outside.

People who are unfamiliar with this feature sometimes accidentally activate the Child Lock while washing, cleaning, or repairing their car. When this happens, they may find that the rear door cannot be opened from inside and mistakenly take the car to a workshop for inspection.

The Child Lock was mainly designed for children. When children are sitting in the rear seats without an adult, they may accidentally open the car door out of curiosity. This can put their lives at risk and may even lead to serious accidents.

Using the Child Lock is simple. In some vehicles, the Child Lock is operated by moving a switch up or down, with the down position usually indicating that it is locked. In some cars, it can be activated or deactivated using the car key. Other vehicles have an electronic control system, allowing the driver to activate or deactivate the Child Lock using a button.

Therefore, whenever you find that a car door cannot be opened from the inside but can be opened from the outside, remember to check the Child Lock before assuming there is a problem with the door.
