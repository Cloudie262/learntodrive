Why Does the ABS Warning Light Come On?

The ABS (Anti-Lock Brake System) is an important safety system in a vehicle. It consists of components such as wheel speed sensors (ABS speed sensors), the ABS motor, ABS actuator, ABS control unit, and wiring. When there is a fault in the ABS system, the ABS warning light on the dashboard comes on to alert the driver.

In this article, we would like to share some useful information about the common problems that can cause the ABS warning light to come on.

(1) Low Brake Fluid
Brake fluid is used as the hydraulic fluid in the vehicle’s braking system. Low brake fluid can be caused by worn brake pads or brake shoes, leaks in the brake lines, a faulty brake servo/vacuum system, or brake master cylinder fluid leakage. When the brake fluid level becomes too low, it can cause the ABS warning light to come on.

(2) Faulty ABS Sensor
The Wheel Speed Sensor, also known as the ABS sensor, is installed near the wheel hub and detects the rotation of the vehicle’s wheels. A sensor is installed for each wheel.

When an ABS sensor becomes faulty, the ABS warning light may come on. In some vehicles, the ABS sensor can be replaced separately. However, in other vehicles, the sensor is integrated with the wheel hub/bearing assembly, so the hub and sensor may need to be replaced together.

(3) Faulty ABS Module
A faulty ABS module, sometimes simply called the ABS unit, can also cause the ABS warning light to come on. The ABS module contains solenoids and a motor, so problems such as a faulty motor, weak solenoids, or damaged solenoids can cause ABS faults.

One of the possible causes of ABS module failure is not replacing the brake fluid regularly.

(4) Damaged Hydraulic Pump
Some vehicles have a brake hydraulic pump as part of the braking system. Particularly in vehicles equipped with an EBD (Electronic Brakeforce Distribution) system, a faulty hydraulic pump can cause the ABS warning light to illuminate.

Because the vehicle’s braking system is extremely important for safety, you should not ignore the ABS warning light. It is recommended to have the vehicle inspected and repaired at a qualified workshop as soon as possible.

Sometimes, the ABS warning light can also come on because water has entered the ABS sensor, the sensor wiring is damaged, or a brake light has burned out, depending on the vehicle’s system.
