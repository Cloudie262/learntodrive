Signs That Your Engine Oil Is Running Low

Engine oil is used in a car’s lubrication system and must be maintained at the specified level. As the oil circulates between the engine’s moving components, it helps prevent wear, excessive heat, and corrosion. In this article, we would like to share some useful information about the common signs that can help you easily recognize when your engine oil level is getting low.

(1) Oil warning light comes on
Cars have an Oil Pressure Warning Light on the dashboard to indicate problems related to engine oil. When the engine oil pressure becomes too low for various reasons, the oil warning light will illuminate in red on the dashboard. If this happens, you should stop the engine immediately and check the engine oil level.

Although the engine oil level can decrease slightly even when there is no leakage, the amount should normally be small. If you check the oil level using the dipstick and find that the oil level has dropped significantly, you should carefully inspect the engine for oil leaks and have the problem repaired.

(2) Burning oil smell
Damaged oil seals or leaks in areas where engine oil is contained can cause a significant amount of oil to leak out. When leaking engine oil comes into contact with hot engine components, it can produce a burning smell. Therefore, if you notice a burning oil smell, you should check the engine oil level because the oil may be leaking and running low.

(3) Engine becomes noisier or rougher
When the engine oil level is low, less oil circulates between the moving components, which can cause the engine to become noisier or rougher. This can also be caused by the condition or quality of the engine oil, so it is important to check the oil level and condition.

The signs mentioned above are some of the most common symptoms that occur when the engine oil level becomes low. Driving with insufficient engine oil can cause serious engine damage, so the oil level should be checked regularly. If the engine oil level suddenly drops, you should have the car inspected at a workshop as soon as possible to determine the cause of the oil loss and repair the problem.
